Sustainability that's good for business, not just the brochure

For small firms, the best green steps also cut costs. Where to begin without greenwashing.

By Aroha Ngata14 October 20265 min read

Sustainability can feel like something only big companies with big budgets do. In practice, the most effective first steps for a small business are the ones that also save money — which makes them easy to justify and easy to keep.

Start where it pays for itself

Cutting energy use, reducing waste, and choosing durable or local suppliers often lowers costs at the same time as your footprint. Measure what you use before you change it, so you can see the difference and tell the story honestly.

And honesty matters. Kiwi customers are quick to spot greenwashing. Make real changes, talk about them plainly, and let your actions do the convincing — authentic effort builds far more trust than a polished claim you can't back up.
