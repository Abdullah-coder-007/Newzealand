Build a compliance calendar and never miss a deadline

Tax dates, filing, health and safety reviews — one simple calendar keeps the whole lot off your mind.

By Mere Tupou6 October 20264 min read

Small-business owners carry the heaviest compliance load relative to their size, and most of the stress comes from not knowing what's due when. A compliance calendar fixes that in an afternoon.

List every recurring obligation — provisional and terminal tax dates, GST returns, PAYE, annual accounts, ACC, insurance renewals, and any licences or health-and-safety reviews your trade requires. Put each one in a shared calendar with a reminder a fortnight before.

Compliance isn't hard. Remembering it on top of everything else is. So don't rely on memory.

Once it's set up, your obligations arrive as calm reminders rather than nasty surprises — and if you ever bring on staff or an accountant, the whole picture is already written down.
